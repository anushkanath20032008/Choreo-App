Questions owners ask.

Do parents need to install an app?

No. They get a WhatsApp message with a link, pay by UPI and receive a receipt.

Can you move my data from a register or spreadsheet?

Yes. Send a spreadsheet or a photo of your register and we enter it for you.

How do the reminders work?

You pick the timing, for example three days before the due date and a nudge four days after. Each message includes a payment link.

We have several trainers. Is that covered?

Today the app is built around one studio team. Tick "multiple trainers" when you book and tell us how you work. It shapes what we build next.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time. We hand you all your students, attendance and fees as a spreadsheet.
